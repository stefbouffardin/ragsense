Data Sources

Connect and index any knowledge source

Ragsense integrates with all your existing tools and repositories — Drupal, SharePoint, Notion, Slack, Git and more. Automated indexing keeps your AI knowledge base always current, without manual intervention.

Structured and unstructured data, documents, sitemaps, APIs — everything flows into a single, always-up-to-date intelligence layer.

Monitoring

Full visibility into AI usage and costs

Track every AI interaction, monitor LLM spending in real time, and understand how your teams are leveraging knowledge AI. Enterprise-grade observability with detailed analytics dashboards built in from day one.

From daily question volumes to cost per query, Ragsense gives you the data you need to optimize and scale confidently.

Transparency

Understand exactly how your AI answers

Ragsense is the only AI platform that lets you visualize and inspect the exact data chunks sent to the LLM to generate each answer. No black boxes — full traceability at every step.

Essential for regulated industries, GDPR compliance, and any organization that cannot afford unexplained AI outputs.

Content Management

Upload, manage and control your content

Easily upload documents in any format, configure ingestion pipelines, and manage your entire knowledge base from a single interface. Role-based access control and audit logs included.

Trigger re-indexing on demand, schedule automated ingestion, or set up real-time sync — all without touching a line of code.

AI Agents

Deploy AI agents tailored to your brand

Launch conversational AI agents designed for your specific use cases — customer service, internal helpdesk, sales enablement, or any custom workflow. No-code configuration, enterprise-grade reliability.

Every agent is fully customizable: choose your LLM, define the tone of voice, restrict knowledge scope, and deploy in minutes across web, mobile, or API.

Review Pipeline

Powerful collaborative conversation reviews

Get real time access to anonymized GDPR compliant conversation records with user comments. Escalate issues to expert reviewers or development team. Create test cases and empower machine learning by adding new conversation scenarios.
